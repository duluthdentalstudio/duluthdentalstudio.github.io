About Us

About Duluth Dental Studio

Duluth Dental Studio is a family dental practice located in Duluth, Georgia, offering care for patients of all ages. The team provides general, cosmetic, restorative, pediatric, and emergency dentistry, along with services like clear aligners, teeth whitening, dental implants, and sedation dentistry, all under one roof.

Led by Dr. Craig Martinson, who has practiced dentistry for over 30 years, the practice combines experience with a modern, comfortable office and advanced dental technology. Same-day appointments are available for patients who call before noon, and new patients can take advantage of a low-cost emergency exam or a free second opinion consultation.

Our Mission

At Duluth Dental Studio, our mission is to provide exceptional dental care in a friendly, comfortable, and stress-free environment for patients of every age. We are committed to treating each person with honesty, compassion, and respect, taking the time to listen to their concerns and explain their treatment options clearly.

We believe quality dental care should be accessible and transparent, which is why we focus on clear communication about treatments and pricing at every step. Our goal goes beyond treating teeth — we want to build lasting relationships with our patients and their families, helping them maintain healthy, confident smiles for life.
