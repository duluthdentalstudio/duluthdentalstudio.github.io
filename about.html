About Us

Get to know Duluth Dental Studio

A family dental home built on experience, honesty, and personal attention.

Welcome to Duluth Dental Studio, a family-friendly dental practice dedicated to providing exceptional care for patients of all ages. Located in Duluth, Georgia, we are committed to delivering efficient, empathetic, and expert dental care at every visit, in an environment where patients feel comfortable, respected, and well cared for.

Who We Are

Meet Dr. Craig Martinson

Our practice is led by Dr. Craig Martinson, who has practiced dentistry for more than 30 years. Dr. Martinson earned his degree in biology from the University of Georgia before completing his dental education at the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry. His experience, combined with a genuine commitment to patient comfort, has helped shape Duluth Dental Studio into a trusted dental home for the local community.

Alongside Dr. Martinson, our team of dental professionals brings years of hands-on experience, ensuring every patient receives care from skilled and attentive hands.

Our Approach

Care that's personal, not one-size-fits-all

Every patient who walks through our doors has different needs, concerns, and goals, and we take the time to understand them before recommending any treatment. We listen closely, answer questions thoroughly, and build customized treatment plans that align with each patient's oral health and lifestyle.

Whether you're visiting for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, our goal is to make every appointment a positive, informative experience.

Our Environment

A modern, comfortable office

We understand that visiting the dentist isn't always easy for everyone, which is why we've designed our office to be clean, modern, and welcoming from the moment you walk in. Combined with advanced dental technology, our facility allows us to provide accurate diagnoses and effective treatment planning.

Full-Service Care

Comprehensive services for the whole family

From general and cosmetic dentistry to restorative treatments, pediatric care, and emergency services, Duluth Dental Studio offers a full range of services under one roof. This includes clear aligners, teeth whitening, dental implants, gum disease therapy, and sedation dentistry for patients who feel anxious about visiting the dentist. Offering this wide range of services allows patients and their families to receive consistent, high-quality care without needing to visit multiple providers for different needs.

Honest, Transparent Care

We believe trust is built through honesty. That's why we prioritize clear communication about treatment options and pricing, ensuring patients always understand their choices before moving forward with care. There are no surprises, just straightforward guidance from a team that has your best interests in mind.

We also offer a free second opinion consultation for patients who want additional clarity before committing to treatment, along with same-day appointments for those dealing with sudden dental pain or emergencies.

Serving Duluth

Over the years, Duluth Dental Studio has become a familiar and trusted name for families throughout Duluth and the surrounding areas. We take pride in the relationships we've built with our patients, many of whom have been visiting our practice for years and continue to bring their families along with them.

Our commitment to you

Our mission goes beyond treating teeth. We aim to build lasting relationships with our patients, support healthy smiles for life, and make dental care a positive, approachable part of your routine.
